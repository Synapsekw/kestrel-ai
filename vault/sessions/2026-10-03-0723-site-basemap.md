## Why

- The Overview's Location pane was an outline on an empty background. The operator asked for an
  open-source map that needs no keys. OpenLayers was already in the app, so the real question was
  the tile source. A backend cache keeps the CSP unchanged and lets a site that was opened once
  still work offline.

## How to test

1. Open Kestrel AI (installed from `31a1a5ab`) with the internet on.
2. Open a project that has a map, a point cloud or GPS photos, then go to **Overview**.
3. **Location** (bottom right) shows satellite imagery under the dashed outline, photo dots and
   pins, with "© Esri, Maxar, Earthstar Geographics" in the corner.
4. Click **Map**: the OpenStreetMap street map appears and the credit changes to "© OpenStreetMap contributors".
5. Reopen the project: **Map** is still selected.
6. Go offline and reopen the same project: the tiles still show (from the cache).
7. Offline, open a project never opened before: the plain outline appears, as before. No error, no delay.
