## Why

CI had been red on every run, so nobody read it, and four causes had stacked up behind one another.
A failed step skips the rest, so pytest had never run on CI and `sidecar-smoke` had never run at
all. The local gate was a subset of CI, which is how every merge passed locally and failed there.
Three of the e2e "flakes" turned out to be real bugs where the app ignores user input: two in the
editor, one in the train form.
